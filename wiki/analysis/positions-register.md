# Positions Register

> [!note] Template page — delete this note once you have your first real row.
> Leave this page mostly empty until the wiki starts taking *positions* — working conclusions a wrong answer would be costly on (usually after the first handful of ingests). Then enumerate them here. **Inclusion test:** would being wrong about this reshape the project or cost something serious? Keep it to ~10 rows — if everything's a position, nothing is. Goals and preferences are *tiebreakers* and live in CLAUDE.md; positions are *beliefs about the world* serving them.

> [!abstract] What this page is for
> The wiki has conclusions on many pages but no *inventory of beliefs* without this page. The register enumerates the load-bearing positions, labels how hard each one is (the same claim reads equally authoritative whether it's settled-after-challenge or a stand-in restated 40 times), records the **strongest ingested counter-argument**, and pre-registers **what evidence would change our minds** — written while calm, before sunk costs and motivated reasoning supply answers.

## How to use this page

- **At ingest:** if a source bears on a row, update the relevant cell — especially "strongest against." A source that *agrees* with a position must still contribute its best counter-claim (the steelman-at-ingest rule in CLAUDE.md), or an explicit "contained none." New evidence lands here before it counts as mind-changing.
- **Settled ≠ relitigatable.** A 🟢 row says "this survived challenge on these grounds — don't reopen without new evidence of the *specific kind* its falsification cell names." Half this page's value is *stopping* wasteful re-argument; the other half is making genuine reversals visible.
- **An empty "strongest against" cell is a flagged reading assignment** — you're inside a bubble on that position; commission the opposing source deliberately. Scan for empty cells at the annual review.

## Status legend

| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **settled-after-challenge** | Survived an adversarial source or explicit steelman; reopen only on the named falsification evidence |
| 🟡 **working-default** | Best current answer, lightly tested; expected to move as analysis deepens |
| 🟠 **assumption** | A modeling stand-in that hardens through repetition — flagged so restatement doesn't masquerade as evidence |

## Maintenance rules

1. **Rows are added** when a new belief becomes load-bearing — typically at ingest or after a major analysis page.
2. **Rows are retired** to an archive section when the underlying decision executes or the position becomes moot — with a line recording how it resolved and whether the original confidence was warranted (the calibration record).
3. **Annual review:** scan for empty/stale "strongest against" cells (reading assignments), statuses that no longer match reality (🟠 quietly treated as 🟢 elsewhere), and falsification criteria that have silently fired. Tally the year's genuine reversals — the scoreboard for whether the wiki is *updating* or just accumulating.
4. **This page restates no figures** — links carry the specifics per the sensitive-info convention, so register rows don't become a second place for values to go stale.
